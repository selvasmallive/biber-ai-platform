# The plan: T1 for tax year 2027, T2 for mid-2027

Decided 2026-10-17. Supersedes the November 2026 T2 target.

The calendar below was established from CRA's published pages the same day and
is recorded in full, with sources, in `CRA-CERTIFICATION.md`. This document is
the plan built on it.

## The correction that makes this plan different

Every previous plan in this repository measured against **"CRA distributes
certification test returns in mid-November"**. That is a real, published fact —
about the **T1 EFILE programme**. It had been applied to a T2 product, and it
does not describe the T2 at all.

CRA publishes no developer-facing T2 calendar. Its Corporation Internet Filing
certified-software list carries **no tax year**: no year heading, no "certified
tax years" column, no cycle, no developer deadline. The only four-digit number
on the page is the date it was last modified. The T1 list, by contrast, is
headed "Certified tax software for 2025" and has a year column. They are
different concepts.

What CRA grants for a T2 is a **product version certified for a window of
corporate tax year ends**. Vendors ship two rounds a year:

| Round | Released | Covers year ends through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | May–June | 31 October |
| Autumn | October–November | 31 May |

So **"T2 mid-2027" is a real round, not a wish** — the spring 2027 round,
≈ mid-May 2027, covering year ends to 31 October 2027. The equivalent round a
year earlier was certified 11 May 2026. The month is well evidenced by two
independent vendors across two years; the date is not published by CRA and is
not a commitment.

This is a better-mapped target than the November 2026 date it replaces, which
was never a T2 date at all.

## Two tracks

### T2 — spring 2027 round, ≈ May 2027

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Target | Corporate year ends through 31 Oct 2027, Ontario |
| Services | Corporation Internet Filing + T2 Barcode, as applied for |
| Build against | `t2-10-26e` (published 2026-10-15) |

**Nothing is waiting on CRA.** The T2 figures that matter — the $500,000
business limit, the taxable-capital and investment-income grind thresholds, the
Part IV and refundable tax rates — are statutory, not indexed. Every schedule
the missing arithmetic needs (3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 23/49, 53, 100/125, 141) is
published today. This is the single most important scheduling fact on this
page: **the T2 engine work has no external dependency and can start now.**

Remaining arithmetic, largest first: capital gains; Part IV tax and the
dividend refund; aggregate investment income, additional refundable tax and
RDTOH; loss continuity; Schedule 5 provincial allocation; the full Schedules
100/125; Schedules 50/53/141; the T2 bar code. Both business-limit grinds are
in progress.

### T1 — tax year 2027

| Milestone | Date | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developer application | ~mid-July 2027 | **weak** — see below |
| 2027 indexed amounts published | Nov–Dec 2026 | pattern |
| Confidentiality agreement, site access | before Nov 2027 | firm |
| Test returns distributed | mid-November 2027 | firm — "each year" |
| Forms public | December 2027 | firm |
| NETFILE opens | **Mon 21 Feb 2028** | extrapolated |

The February date extrapolates from three firm openings — 19 Feb 2024,
24 Feb 2025, 23 Feb 2026, always a Monday. The Monday inside that span in 2028
is the 21st.

**The mid-July application deadline is the weakest-sourced date in this plan.**
Its only evidence is a 2016 developer mailing-list thread. It is also the one
that decides whether the two tracks collide, since mid-July 2027 sits in the
same quarter as the spring T2 round. Pin it before committing to either.

The 2027 amounts arriving Nov–Dec 2026 are the near-term dependency worth
watching: they unblock the 2027 parameter pack, and `SUPPORTED_YEARS` cannot
include 2027 until they exist.

## Sequencing

**Now — nothing blocks it**

1. T2 arithmetic, in the order above. This is the long pole and it has no
   external dependency.
2. The four T1 gaps the 2025 federal audit found: the Top-Up Tax Credit
   (line 34990, absent entirely), Schedule 9's 33% donation tier, the CWB
   disability family phase-out, the secondary-earner exemption, and the
   under-18 disability supplement. Up to ~$2,000 on a single return.
3. Code-signing certificates — Windows OV/EV, Apple Developer ID and
   notarization. Weeks of lead time, and **until the Apple certificate exists
   there is no macOS product at all**, because Gatekeeper refuses rather than
   warns. macOS is a separately declared channel.

**When the 2027 amounts publish (Nov–Dec 2026)**

4. Build and verify the 2027 parameter packs; add 2027 to `SUPPORTED_YEARS`.

**Before the spring 2027 T2 round**

5. The CIF transmission adapter. Blocked on the confidentiality agreement and
   controlled-site access, which are themselves blocked on item 1 of the
   escalations below.

## Escalations — not mine to decide

1. **The 2 Sept 2026 T2 application has had no recorded reply, and is now six
   weeks old.** Whether it lapses, carries forward, or must be restated when
   the target moves is **not published by CRA for either return type**. It
   changes the plan and it goes to the government. This is the first thing to
   resolve and nothing else should be sequenced before it.

2. **The web channel is not served by anything.** The licence service has no
   static-file layer, `DEPLOY.md` has no web entry, and the bundle and engine
   are both build output. "Freshet T2 Online" is a declared channel that exists
   only under `npm run dev`. Either deploy it or tell CRA it is deferred.

3. **Code signing costs money.** See item 3 above for why it cannot wait.

## Two things worth naming plainly

**A T2 round is in progress right now.** `t2-10-26e` was published 2026-10-15
and vendors are shipping against it this month, covering year ends to 31 May
2027. Deferring to mid-2027 means standing out of a second consecutive round.
That is a choice rather than a missed deadline, and it is the right one — but
it should be made with open eyes.

**Deferring buys engineering time and nothing else.** It moves neither the
confidentiality agreement nor controlled-site access, so it does not advance
the CIF adapter by a single day. Those are gated on escalation 1.
